# Velora Product Vision

## Product Name

Velora AI Business Analyst


## Mission

Help business owners understand their company data and make better decisions using AI.


## Problem

Business owners have:

- Excel files
- PDF reports
- Sales data
- Customer information
- Business reports

But they don't know:

- Where money is being lost
- What opportunities exist
- What problems need attention
- What actions to take next


## Solution

Velora allows users to:

1. Upload business files
2. Analyze company data
3. Discover problems
4. Find opportunities
5. Receive an action plan


## Core Features

- Business file upload
- AI analysis
- Executive summary
- Problem detection
- Opportunity discovery
- Action recommendations


## MVP Goal

Create a simple AI business analyst that delivers useful insights from business documents.


## Long Term Vision

Build Velora into an AI Operating System for Businesses.
