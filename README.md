# Velora

> Know what to do next.

Velora is an AI-powered Business Analyst built for small and medium businesses.

## Vision

Build the AI Operating System for Business.

## Problem

Business owners have data in Excel, PDF, and reports but struggle to answer:

- Why are sales changing?
- Where is money being lost?
- What should I do next?

## Solution

Velora helps users:

- Upload business files
- Analyze business data with AI
- Ask questions about their business
- Receive clear recommendations
- Export a PDF report

## MVP Features

- File Upload
- AI Business Analysis
- Business Q&A
- PDF Report Generation

🚧 MVP in active development.
