# Velora AI Business Analyst Report

## Overview

Velora analyzes business files and provides clear business insights.

## Supported Files

- Excel (.xlsx)
- PDF (.pdf)
- Word (.docx)

## Analysis Output

Velora provides:

- Executive Summary
- Problems Detection
- Business Opportunities
- Next Actions
