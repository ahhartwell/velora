# Velora Product Requirements Document

## Product

Velora Analyst

## Goal

Help business owners understand their business data using AI.

## Target Users

- Small businesses
- Agencies
- E-commerce stores
- Freelancers

## MVP Features

1. Upload PDF files
2. Upload Excel files
3. AI Business Analysis
4. Ask questions about business data
5. Generate PDF report

## Success Criteria

- Users can upload files.
- AI generates useful business insights.
- Users can export a PDF report.
